# German Connectors — English Meanings and Word Order

A quick reference for German conjunctions and connecting adverbs. The English translations are approximate; context can change the best translation.

## 1. Coordinating conjunctions — normal main-clause word order

These conjunctions do not send the conjugated verb to the end. The main clause retains normal verb-second order.

| Connector | English meaning | German example | English translation |
|---|---|---|---|
| **und** | and | Ich lerne Deutsch und ich höre Musik. | I learn German and I listen to music. |
| **oder** | or | Ich trinke Tee oder ich trinke Kaffee. | I drink tea or I drink coffee. |
| **aber** | but | Ich bin müde, aber ich lerne. | I am tired, but I study. |
| **denn** | because / for | Ich bleibe zu Hause, denn ich bin krank. | I stay home because I am sick. |
| **sondern** | but rather / instead | Ich trinke keinen Tee, sondern Kaffee. | I don't drink tea, but rather coffee. |

**Note:** *sondern* usually contrasts with a preceding negation (nicht/kein).

## 2. Subordinating conjunctions — conjugated verb at the end

These introduce subordinate clauses. The conjugated verb normally goes to the end of the subordinate clause.

| Connector | English meaning | German example | English translation |
|---|---|---|---|
| **weil** | because | Ich bleibe zu Hause, weil ich krank bin. | I stay home because I am sick. |
| **dass** | that | Ich glaube, dass Deutsch schwierig ist. | I think that German is difficult. |
| **obwohl** | although / even though | Ich lerne, obwohl ich müde bin. | I study although I am tired. |
| **wenn** | if / when (repeated or present/future) | Wenn ich Zeit habe, lerne ich Deutsch. | When/if I have time, I study German. |
| **ob** | whether / if (indirect question) | Ich weiß nicht, ob er kommt. | I don't know whether he is coming. |
| **damit** | so that / in order that | Ich lerne Deutsch, damit ich einen Job finden kann. | I learn German so that I can find a job. |
| **bevor** | before | Ich trinke Wasser, bevor ich schlafen gehe. | I drink water before I go to sleep. |
| **nachdem** | after | Nachdem ich gegessen habe, gehe ich spazieren. | After I have eaten, I go for a walk. |
| **während** | while | Ich höre Musik, während ich koche. | I listen to music while I cook. |
| **falls** | in case / if | Falls es regnet, bleibe ich zu Hause. | If it rains, I'll stay home. |
| **seitdem** | since / ever since | Seitdem ich hier wohne, lerne ich Deutsch. | Since I moved here / have been living here, I have been learning German. |
| **bis** | until | Ich warte, bis du kommst. | I wait until you come. |
| **als** | when (one-time event/state in the past) | Als ich klein war, spielte ich Fußball. | When I was little, I played football. |

**Modal verb pattern:** In a subordinate clause, the infinitive comes before the conjugated modal verb: **damit ich ein Auto kaufen kann** (so that I can buy a car).

**Subordinate clause first:** When the subordinate clause comes first, the main clause starts with its conjugated verb: **Wenn ich Zeit habe, lerne ich Deutsch.**

**After work — useful expression:** **Ich bin mit der Arbeit fertig** = “I am finished with work.” Here *mit* takes the dative, so feminine *die Arbeit* becomes *mit der Arbeit*. A useful sentence is **Wenn ich mit der Arbeit fertig bin, esse ich zu Abend** (“When I finish work, I eat dinner”). With *nachdem*, a clearly completed previous action can be expressed as **Nachdem ich die Arbeit beendet habe, esse ich zu Abend** (“After I have finished work, I eat dinner”).

## 3. Connecting adverbs — verb second, immediately after a sentence-initial connector

These are not subordinating conjunctions. When they start a main clause, the conjugated verb comes right after them, followed by the subject in many simple examples.

| Connector | English meaning | German example | English translation |
|---|---|---|---|
| **deshalb** | therefore / that's why | Ich bin krank. Deshalb bleibe ich zu Hause. | I am sick. That's why I stay home. |
| **deswegen** | therefore / for that reason | Ich bin müde. Deswegen schlafe ich. | I am tired. That's why I sleep. |
| **trotzdem** | nevertheless / despite that | Es regnet. Trotzdem gehe ich spazieren. | It is raining. Nevertheless, I go for a walk. |
| **außerdem** | furthermore / besides | Ich lerne Deutsch. Außerdem lerne ich Englisch. | I learn German. Besides that, I learn English. |
| **danach** | afterward / after that | Ich esse. Danach gehe ich schlafen. | I eat. After that, I go to sleep. |
| **dann** | then | Ich mache meine Hausaufgaben. Dann sehe ich fern. | I do my homework. Then I watch TV. |

## Three patterns to remember

1. **Coordinating:** *Ich bin müde, aber ich lerne.* — normal main-clause word order.
2. **Subordinating:** *Ich bleibe zu Hause, weil ich krank bin.* — conjugated verb at the end of the subordinate clause.
3. **Connecting adverb:** *Ich bin krank. Deshalb bleibe ich zu Hause.* — verb second; after sentence-initial *deshalb* comes *bleibe*.
