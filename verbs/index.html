German verbs · Verben

A desk-friendly digital reference for your German lessons: regular verbs, separable verbs, inseparable verbs, verbs with accusative objects, and verbs with dative objects. Includes example sentences, English meanings and a bonus table for verbs using both cases.

Two different classifications: Regular / separable / inseparable explains conjugation or prefixes; accusative / dative explains the case of a verb’s object. One verb can appear in multiple tables. Do not assume every occurrence of an accusative-list verb must have an object, or that a dative verb cannot take additional complements.

Study tip: Learn five verbs each day with one sentence. Pay special attention to helfen + Dativ, sehen + Akkusativ and the separable form Ich rufe dich an.
